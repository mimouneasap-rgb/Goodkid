Training use. This master class summarises EU GMP Annex 1 (2022) and Annex 15 (2015), ISO 14644 parts 1–4, ICH Q9(R1), FDA's 2004 aseptic processing guidance, WHO TRS 1010 Annex 8, the ISPE Baseline Guide on Commissioning & Qualification (2nd ed.), ASTM E2500 and GAMP 5. Values labelled "typical" reflect common industry practice. Your site's URS, Contamination Control Strategy, SOPs and approved protocols set the actual acceptance criteria. Always check the current edition of each document before you quote it in a protocol.

Risk scores use the same FMEA rules as the Quality Risk Manager: RPN = S × O × D (1–125); level from S × O: Low 1–4, Medium 5–9, High 10–19, Critical 20–25.
