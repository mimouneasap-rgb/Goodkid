Module 2

Qualification lifecycle for a containment facility

SOPVL0102E sets DQ, IQ, OQ and PQ for the HVAC system. A high-potent facility adds three things: a containment master plan and hazard banding at the start (SOPGS0023E), equipment containment tests at FAT and OQ, and a containment verification with a surrogate powder in PQ. Select a stage.

Module 3

Containment bands, site limits and the pressure cascade

The hazard sets the containment; the containment sets the HVAC. SOPGS0023E classifies a product as highly hazardous when its PDE (OEL × 10 m³) is 10 µg/day or lower.

Occupational exposure bands and typical controls

Band boundaries vary between companies and consultants. Your toxicologist assigns the band; equipment containment performance must be verified against it.

Isolator leak tightness

Pressure-change method, ISO 10648-2. Hourly leak rate and class.

Rh = (3600 / t) × |P₂/T₂ − P₁/T₁| / (P₁/T₁), absolute pressures and kelvin.

Exposure limit to hazard category

SOPGS0023E: PDE (µg/day) = OEL (µg/m³) × 10 m³.

8 h TWA = measured × task min / 480. Compare with the OEL.

Door leakage and supply–extract offset

Air needed through door gaps to hold a pressure difference. Negative rooms need extract greater than supply by this amount.

Q = 0.83 × A × ΔP0.5 m³/s per opening (orifice equation).

Air changes and recovery

Theoretical 100:1 recovery corrected for mixing. Compare with the SOPVL0102E recovery test.

ACH = Q / V. t100:1 = 276 / (ACH × ε) min.
